01

What we found

A public review of tipicita-kinesio.com, its Google presence, and the local search landscape around Le Bouscat and Bordeaux.

High impact

Location move is invisible to Google

The site title says "bordeaux" but there is no dedicated page for Le Bouscat. Old citations from Eysines and Mérignac are likely still live across French directories (Pages Jaunes, Resalib, Doctolib), splitting your local authority between three addresses instead of one. Google Business Profile needs a full update and re-verification for the new address.

High impact

No conversion tracking at all

No GA4 events, no call tracking, no form submission tracking. Every booking request, phone call and ebook download goes unmeasured. Without this, there is no way to know which keywords or pages bring real clients, and no way to prove ROI on any work done.

High impact

Missing structured data and local schema

No LocalBusiness or HealthAndBeautyBusiness JSON-LD markup on any page. No FAQ schema. This means Google and AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overview) cannot pull structured information about the practice, services, or location. This directly hurts both traditional rankings and GEO visibility.

Medium impact

Content misses local search intent

The site communicates the method and philosophy well, but does not target the queries people actually search: "kinésiologue Le Bouscat", "coach de vie Bordeaux", "kinésiologie stress anxiété Bordeaux". Blog content exists but is not keyword-targeted. No location-specific landing pages exist.

Medium impact

Wix technical gaps

Old URLs from the Eysines era likely return 404 instead of 301 redirects. Sitemap accuracy, canonical tags, and Core Web Vitals all need auditing. Wix limits some technical SEO controls, but the critical ones (redirects, meta tags, schema injection) are available and need to be configured properly.

Medium impact

Mobile booking friction

Multiple CTAs on the page, which is good. But the booking flow on mobile needs a usability check: click-to-call visibility, form field count, and whether the free discovery call CTA is above the fold on a phone screen. Small fixes here directly increase appointment bookings.

Set up conversion tracking before anything else

Install GA4 with events for booking clicks, discovery call requests, ebook downloads and phone calls. Set up Google Search Console. Without this baseline, every later decision is a guess.

Why: you asked for qualified leads and appointments, not traffic. We need to count them first.

Fix the location migration on Google

Update Google Business Profile to Le Bouscat with correct NAP, categories, services, and photos. Audit every French directory listing (Pages Jaunes, Resalib, Doctolib, Yelp, local health directories) and correct the address everywhere. Set up 301 redirects from any old Eysines/Mérignac URLs.

Why: inconsistent addresses across the web confuse Google about where you actually are.

Add structured data and optimise every page

Inject LocalBusiness and HealthAndBeautyBusiness JSON-LD on all pages. Add FAQ schema targeting "People Also Ask" queries. Optimise title tags, meta descriptions, H1 structure. Create a dedicated "Kinésiologue Le Bouscat" landing page with location-specific content.

Why: schema is what AI assistants and Google pull from. No schema, no visibility in AI results.

Publish keyword-targeted French content

Four articles per month targeting real search queries: "kinésiologue Le Bouscat", "gestion du stress kinésiologie Bordeaux", "coaching de vie vs thérapie", "kinésiologie et burnout". Each article structured for featured snippets and AI citation.

Why: your current blog does not target the words people search. These articles fill that gap.

Build local backlinks and review generation

Submit to French health and wellness directories. Outreach to Bordeaux-area blogs and health publications. Set up an automated post-session review request flow to grow Google reviews at the new location.

Why: reviews and local links are the two strongest local ranking signals after NAP consistency.

Optimise mobile booking and report results

Audit the mobile booking path: simplify forms, test CTA placement, ensure click-to-call is prominent. Build a Looker Studio dashboard showing rankings, traffic, calls, bookings and cost per new client. Monthly report with plain-language findings.

Why: your site already has good CTAs. Small mobile fixes turn more visitors into booked sessions.
